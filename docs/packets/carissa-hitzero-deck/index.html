Competitive analysis

The market already
knows this category.

Competitor set What they sell What it proves for Hit Zero
iClassPro + Jackrabbit Established cheer/class suites: registration, billing, portals, schedules, reporting, skill tracking, branded app options. Cheer gyms already buy software. Hit Zero does not need to create the category; it needs a sharper early wedge.
Studio Pro + Studio Director Studio-style operations: lead management, class scheduling, tuition, payments, communication, attendance, and private lessons. The pain is not niche. Every class-based youth business fights registration, billing, parent comms, and staff visibility.
SportsEngine + TeamSnap + Amilia Broader sports/recreation platforms: registration, payments, schedules, rosters, messaging, websites, reporting, and mobile apps. Big platforms validate the budget, but they are not built from one cheer gym’s lived operating workflow.
ClassJuggler + Gymdesk + Sawyer Lower-cost or adjacent tools for classes, memberships, payment processing, portals, websites, and customer records. There will always be cheaper tools. Hit Zero wins when the buyer wants cheer-native setup, founder speed, and one accountable partner.

Market references checked June 13, 2026: iClassPro, Jackrabbit Class, SportsEngine Motion, Amilia, Studio Pro, The Studio Director, ClassJuggler, TeamSnap, Gymdesk, and Sawyer.
