## Core Positioning

Hit Zero is not just a website, class form, or payment link.

Hit Zero is a cheer-gym operating system that connects the public front door to the actual business workflow: class discovery, family signup, paid registration, Square payment, family packet, parent/athlete linking, staff review, rosters, schedules, athlete records, and owner visibility.

Magic City Athletics is the proof-of-concept gym: a live public website, live class offers, connected Square payment, and a working app stack behind it.

The cleanest one-sentence pitch:

> I built the system that turns a cheer gym from texts, forms, spreadsheets, and payment chasing into one connected operating loop.

The sharper business pitch:

> Hit Zero helps cheer gyms convert interest into paid registrations, reduce admin drag, and run parents, athletes, coaches, owners, classes, rosters, packets, and payments from one vertical platform built for cheer.

## Gym Owner Conversation

### Goal

Get the owner to see Hit Zero as a business tool, not software homework.

### Opening

> I built Hit Zero because cheer gyms have a very specific operations problem. Most gyms are not losing money because they cannot coach. They are losing time and revenue because interest, registration, payment, paperwork, rosters, staff follow-up, and parent communication are scattered across too many places.

### Discovery Questions

1. How do families register today?
2. Where do payments happen?
3. How many signups start but never pay?
4. How do you know who is fully cleared to participate?
5. How do parents get linked to athletes?
6. How many systems are you using for classes, payments, waivers, schedules, and communication?
7. How much of this still depends on you personally?
8. What happens during tryouts, summer camps, or fall launch when volume spikes?
9. What would you change if admin was no longer the bottleneck?

### Owner Pitch

> Hit Zero connects the parts that usually leak. Your public site or class link sends families into a paid registration flow. Payment goes through Square. Staff can review the paid pending registration, see family packet status, link parents and athletes, manage classes, and keep rosters and schedules in one place. The value is not just the app. The value is that your gym stops relying on memory, DMs, spreadsheets, and half-finished forms to run the business.

### Owner Close

> I am looking for a small number of early gyms where we can prove this outside Magic City. The best fit is a gym that wants to grow but knows admin and parent follow-through are already becoming a ceiling. I will personally help onboard, configure, and close the loop because the next proof point matters.

### Owner Offer Options

Option A: Early Gym Pilot

- Setup fee: $1,500 to $5,000
- Monthly platform fee: $300 to $750
- Includes implementation, class setup, Square connection guidance, staff training, and founder support.
- Best for small to mid-size gyms.

Option B: Growth Partner Gym

- Setup fee: $5,000 to $15,000
- Monthly platform fee: $750 to $1,500
- Optional revenue share on paid registrations, camps, clinics, or implementation services.
- Includes deeper workflow customization and co-marketing/case-study rights.

Option C: Strategic Reference Gym

- Discounted or deferred setup.
- Strong case-study agreement, testimonials, metrics sharing, and access to staff feedback.
- Use only when the gym has unusually high credibility or network access.

## Recommended Deal Structure

This needs legal review before signing anything, but the business shape should be:

### Preferred Structure: Operating Partner With Earned Equity

Do not give away 49% on day one just because someone is exciting.

Recommended:

- Andrew: 60% to 70% initial founder stake.
- Partner: 10% to 25% earned equity package.
- Option to expand partner ownership to 40% to 49% only if milestones are hit.
- Cash investment separately documented as either SAFE, convertible note, member loan, or equity purchase.

### Why Not Immediate 50/50

Immediate 50/50 creates risk if the partner underperforms, disappears, changes priorities, or cannot sell.

The person may be highly motivated in conversation but unproven as an operator. The structure should reward real contribution, not projected chemistry.

### Suggested Partner Package

Initial cash:

- $25,000 minimum serious partner buy-in.
- $50,000 stronger target.
- $75,000+ ideal if they want near-equal economics later.

Equity:

- 10% at signing for cash plus commitment.
- Additional 15% to 25% vests over 24 to 36 months.
- Additional milestone equity available for results.

Milestone examples:

- First 3 paying gyms closed.
- First $10,000 monthly recurring revenue.
- First $25,000 monthly recurring revenue.
- 12-month active operating contribution.
- Documented brand/content cadence.
- Successful case studies/testimonials from early gyms.

Profit split:

- Do not start with 50/50 profit split immediately.
- First, maintain operating reserves.
- Second, repay documented partner cash if negotiated as repayable.
- Third, distribute profits according to ownership or agreed waterfall.

Control:

- Andrew controls product, technical roadmap, architecture, and implementation decisions.
- Partner controls brand/content/sales motion within agreed brand guardrails.
- Major decisions require consent: debt, issuing equity, selling company, changing pricing model, hiring executives, taking on large obligations.

Compensation:

- No salary until the business can afford it unless investment explicitly funds founder/operator draw.
- If investment funds draw, define modest monthly owner/operator draws up front.
- Sales commissions can be used for non-owner reps later, but partner economics should mainly come from equity/profit.

Buyback / Clawback:

- Unvested equity is forfeited if the partner leaves.
- Vested equity may be subject to company buyback on departure.
- Include bad-leaver terms for misconduct, non-performance, or competitive activity.

### If They Demand 50/50

Counter with:

> I am open to near-equal economics, but not before we prove the working relationship. I already built the product, live deployment, payment flow, and first proof point. I need a partner who can earn into the front-of-house side through capital, sales, content, and operational execution.

Offer:

- 51/49 target after milestone completion.
- 20% to 25% at close.
- Remainder vests after revenue and contribution milestones.
