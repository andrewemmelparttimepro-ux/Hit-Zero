We are opening Hit Zero to the first 10 founding gyms as free OG partner gyms.

Magic City Athletics is already live. There are 9 founding gym spots left.

Founding gyms receive:

- Hit Zero gym account setup.

An OG gym is not a trial user.

An OG gym is a founding reference gym that gets permanent preferred status for helping build the platform early.

Recommended promise:

Premium future services may still be separate if they require direct cost, custom buildout, high-touch implementation, paid marketing, apparel, payment processing, AI modules, or third-party fees.

## Who This Is For

Hit Zero is best for cheer gyms that:

- Want cleaner registration.
- Want fewer unpaid or incomplete signups.
- Use too many forms, texts, spreadsheets, or disconnected tools.
- Run classes, camps, clinics, teams, privates, school clinics, or events.
- Want parents to have a clearer signup and payment path.
- Want staff to see who is pending, paid, linked, cleared, or incomplete.
- Want to grow without the owner being the only operating system.

This is especially strong for gyms that have real demand but feel administrative drag during tryouts, summer camps, fall launch, evaluations, or team placement.

## What Problem Hit Zero Solves

Most cheer gyms do not have a coaching problem.

They have an operations problem:

- Parents fill out a form but do not pay.
- Payments happen somewhere separate from registration.
- Rosters live in spreadsheets.
- Staff cannot quickly see who is linked, paid, approved, or missing information.
- Owners spend too much time answering status questions.
- Coaches walk into practice without clean operational context.
- Parents are not always sure what step comes next.

Hit Zero connects these pieces into one operating loop.

## What Hit Zero Does

### Public Website To Paid Registration

Families can discover classes or programs from the gym's website and continue into a Hit Zero registration/payment flow.

For paid classes, the goal is simple: payment happens before the registration becomes real.

### Square Payment Connection

Where configured, payment flows through the gym's own Square account.

Hit Zero does not need to become the payment processor. It helps connect registration status to payment status.

### Family And Athlete Setup

Parents can create accounts, request gym access, complete family information, and connect to athletes through staff-approved workflows.

### Staff Review

Staff can review registrations, see payment status, manage pending families, approve links, and keep registration follow-up organized.

### Roster, Schedule, And Program Tools

Hit Zero supports the gym's operating layer: classes, schedules, rosters, athlete profiles, and owner/staff views.

## Discovery Call Script

### Start

> I am not trying to sell you a big software contract today. I am looking for the right early gyms to help prove the platform. If it is a fit, your gym can become one of the founding 10 and keep core access free.

### Ask

1. How do families currently register?
2. How do you collect payment?

4. Where do rosters and class lists live?
5. How do staff know who is approved, paid, linked, or missing paperwork?
6. What is the busiest registration season for you?
7. What part of admin still depends too much on you personally?
8. Does your website currently send parents into a clean registration/payment flow?

## Good Fit / Bad Fit

### Good Fit

- Owner wants operational help.
- Gym has real classes/programs.
- Gym is willing to link from its website.
- Owner or admin will actually log in.
- Staff can provide feedback.
- Gym wants to grow.
- Gym has enough activity to create a useful case study.

### Bad Fit

- Owner wants everything custom before using anything.
- Gym will not link from the website.
- Gym does not want to change any workflow.
- No one will be responsible for admin follow-through.
- They want free software but will not provide feedback or proof.

## Recommended Qualification Rule

Do not give an OG spot away casually.

Use this standard:

> An OG gym must be willing to use Hit Zero in a real public workflow and help prove the model.

If they only want to look around, they can be a demo account, not an OG gym.

## Best Time To Pursue The Public-Facing Partner

The best time to pursue a serious athlete/coach/gym-owner growth partner is after the OG gym story is real enough to sell in one sentence.

Recommended milestone:

- 3 to 5 OG gyms live or actively onboarding.
- At least 1 credible owner testimonial.
- At least 1 before/after admin story.
- Clear screenshots of the workflow.
- Repeatable onboarding checklist.
- Early metrics: registrations, paid flows, classes, families, staff usage, or admin hours saved.

At that point, the partner conversation changes.

Before OG proof:

> I built a product. Help me sell the vision.

After OG proof:

> I built the product, MCA is live, multiple founding gyms are coming on, and there are only a few OG spots left. Help me become the public face and growth engine before this scales.

That is a stronger offer and gives the partner something real to push.
